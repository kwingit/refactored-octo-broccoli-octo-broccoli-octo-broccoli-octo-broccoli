KEYBOARD (WORKS WITHOUT LOOKING)

Space: open the menu, then select what your head points at
Double-Space: recenter
← / →: skip 10 s · Enter: play / pause
Space on empty space: close the menu
Using Chrome on iPhone? Chrome on iOS often blocks the motion sensors, so head pointing won’t work. Open this same link in Safari for the full experience.
